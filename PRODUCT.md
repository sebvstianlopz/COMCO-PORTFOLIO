# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) with plain CSS design tokens, GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Lenis for motion. Built-in Astro i18n routing: Spanish at `/` (default), English at `/en/`. Hosting is undecided (the output is static so it can go to Hostinger, Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages later).

## Users

Owners and managers of **local businesses** (restaurants, cafés, gyms, clinics, salons, shops) who know they need a steady, professional social media presence but have no time, skill or team to run it. They arrive from COMCO's Instagram, a referral or a search. Their job on this site is to decide whether COMCO is the person to hand their social media to, and to book a first call. COMCO is based in one city and works with clients locally and remotely.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio site for COMCO, a social media manager. It has one objective: **get the visitor to book a meeting** (Calendly), after seeing the services and liking them. Secondary actions: follow on Instagram and other socials, send an email, pay an invoice via a PayPal.me link. Success is a booked call.

## Positioning

COMCO covers the whole thing end to end, so a local business does not need an agency plus a photographer plus a strategist:
- **Strategy and results**: decisions driven by data (reach, followers, leads), strategy before content.
- **Content creation**: COMCO shoots and edits the content himself (reels, photos, design), with a strong creative eye.
- **Done for you**: planning, posting, community management and reporting, all taken off the owner's plate.

## Operating Context

The visitor is often on a phone, coming from an Instagram bio link, between tasks at their business. Booking happens through an embedded Calendly widget. Payment happens outside the site, through a PayPal.me link, after the work has been agreed (there are no prices or checkout on the site).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual Spanish / English with a visible language toggle. Spanish is the default.
- Calendly inline embed and a popup opened from every booking button.
- A single PayPal.me link (for paying invoices), plus email, Instagram and other social links.
- Undecided: COMCO's exact services list, any prices, city name, which other socials beyond Instagram, Calendly URL, PayPal.me handle, email address.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **COMCO**.
- The user pinned a **dark editorial** look that feels premium, alive, minimal and engaging.
- COMCO is referred to as he/him (stated by the user).

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. The user will provide the services, client results and case studies, testimonials, photos of COMCO, the logo and the social links. Until then, every service, metric, testimonial, client name and photo on the site is a **labeled placeholder**. Do not present invented numbers, clients or quotes as real.

## Product Principles

1. Every section moves the visitor one step closer to booking a call.
2. Show the work, not adjectives: content and results do the persuading.
3. Speak to a busy local business owner: plain language, clear outcomes, no marketing jargon.
4. Fast and calm on a phone first; motion supports attention and never delays content.
5. Both languages are first-class; neither reads like a translation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: text contrast on the dark ground, visible keyboard focus, full keyboard access to the booking buttons and language toggle, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for all motion.
