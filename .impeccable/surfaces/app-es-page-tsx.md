# Surface: COMCO home (ES `/`, EN `/en`)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: local business owners (restaurants, cafés, gyms, clinics, salons, shops), often on a phone from an Instagram bio link. Action: book a free 30-minute call (Calendly, inline + popup). Secondary: Instagram/socials, email, PayPal.me "pay invoice". Proof: placeholders until the user supplies real services, results, testimonials and photos; every metric is labelled as example.

## Direction contract

THESIS: COMCO's offer is an object you open. Every service and every client case is a smoked-glass folder whose contents rise and fan out when touched. The page refuses the category default of a headline over a row of icon cards and floating phone mockups.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite ground, never pure black. Smoked-glass panes with white hairline rims, backdrop blur and a bottom-weighted dark tint. All colour comes from COMCO's own generated liquid artwork (cobalt streak, coral marble, plum marble) seen behind or through glass. Off-white ink. Funnel Display for headlines, Funnel Sans for text. Off-white pill for the primary action; glass pill for secondary.

STORY: The visitor understands that COMCO runs a local business's social media end to end (strategy, content, management). They believe it by opening folders that show deliverables, examples and results. They act by booking a free call, offered at every section's close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed liquid-art back panel (inset, large radius) fills the viewport. A large smoked-glass front pane covers its lower ~60%, with a folder tab at its top-left reading COMCO. Three post "files" (reel, carousel, story) peek above the pane's top edge and rise on hover and focus. The headline sits on the glass, bottom-left, at display scale. Beside it: the primary pill "Reserva una llamada gratis", the secondary "Ver servicios", and "30 min · sin compromiso". A thin glass header runs across the top.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — user: "This is the prompt i want for the portfolio. Dont make it exactly like that, use it for inspirations." (the glass-folder-cards component). Signature interaction: the hinge-and-rise of folders and of the hero files. Motion grammar: spring hinge, exponential ease-out rise, content visible by default.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
